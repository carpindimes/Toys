# 04 — Keeping Images Ad-Safe (Meta / Instagram / Facebook)

The photos the team takes will mostly run as **Meta ads**. Meta rejects ads, and can restrict the ad account, for imagery it considers **shocking, sensational or excessively violent.** One rejected ad costs a day. Repeated rejections can block the account. So we filter for this *at the camera*.

> Meta's Advertising Standards ban "shocking, sensational or excessively violent content." Its violent and graphic content policy says content deemed "sensitive or disturbing is not eligible to run in ads," and specifically includes **depictions of partially or fully uncovered deceased humans**, even when there are no visible signs of violent death.

---

## 🚫 Never in frame (these will get the ad rejected)

- Blood, open wounds, burns, amputations or visible injuries
- Dead bodies, covered or uncovered, including shrouded bodies and body bags
- Medical procedures in progress (surgery, wound dressing)
- Weapons, armed people, explosions, fire with people in danger
- People in extreme distress *in the act* of an injury
- Severely emaciated bodies shown unclothed (also a dignity issue)
- Nudity of any kind, including children being bathed

## ⚠️ Shoot with care (usually OK if framed well)

| Situation | How to keep it ad-safe |
|---|---|
| A child in hospital | Show the face, the parent's hand and the bed. **Keep bandages and wounds out of frame.** |
| Malnutrition | Show the face, the eyes and the empty bowl, not an exposed body. Keep clothing on. |
| Grief | Faces and embraces, **never the deceased** or graves with bodies. |
| Destroyed buildings | Fine. Rubble, ruins and damaged streets are core to our story. Just no visible human remains. |
| Crowds at aid points | Fine, but avoid chaotic crushes, people fighting or anyone hurt. Find one calm face. |
| Tears | Fine. Emotion is not graphic. |

## ✅ Always safe and powerful

- Faces: emotion, eyes, expressions
- Hands: holding bread, water, aid parcels, each other
- People among the ruins: walking, sitting, cooking, children playing
- Aid moments: receiving food, water, blankets; cooking at community kitchens
- Tents and shelters: families inside, with their daily routines

## The "family table" test

> **Would this image be acceptable on the front page of a newspaper at a family breakfast table?**

If yes, it's very likely ad-safe. If you hesitate, reframe: move closer to the face, or step aside to cut the graphic element out of the frame.
