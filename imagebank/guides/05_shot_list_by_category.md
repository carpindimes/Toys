# 05 — Shot List by Category (Action Directions)

Use this as a field checklist. Each line is a direction: **verb first, then what to capture.** Always shoot vertical (9:16), and send 3–5 variations of each scene.

---

## 1 — Dire Need
*Message: "This is urgent. Someone needs you now."*

- **WAIT** with the queue: frame one child holding an empty pot, looking up toward the food point. Let the line blur behind them.
- **KNEEL** at a tent opening: a mother cradling her baby inside, lit by the doorway.
- **ISOLATE** one face in the crowd at the water point. Shallow focus; everyone else is a blur.
- **CAPTURE** hands: small fingers gripping an empty bowl, a jerry can, a parent's sleeve.
- **FRAME** a family huddled under blankets in a damp tent in winter. Show the breath, the rain or the mud.
- **FOLLOW** a child carrying water jugs that are too heavy. Shoot from low, in front, as they walk toward you.
- **HOLD** on an elderly face. The lines, the eyes, the scarf. Tight portrait, eyes in the upper third.
- **SHOW** scale: a small child standing alone in front of a collapsed building, with the ruins filling the frame above them.

## 2 — Life Among the Ruins
*Message: "These are people like you, living through the unthinkable."*

- **CATCH** children playing: running through the rubble, flying a kite, laughing on a broken slab.
- **WATCH** someone cooking on an open fire beside a destroyed home: steam, smoke, a face lit by flames.
- **TRACK** a father walking his children down a street of ruins. Shoot from behind or in front, with the buildings rising either side.
- **FIND** the routine: a girl doing homework in a tent, a man fixing a bicycle, a woman hanging laundry between broken walls.
- **LOOK** through a gap: a broken window or doorway framing a person inside the remains of their home.
- **WAIT** for golden hour: warm light raking across the rubble and a silhouette on top of it.
- **CAPTURE** the small humanities: a shared cup of tea, siblings holding hands, a mother braiding hair.
- **CONTRAST** colour against grey: a bright scarf, a red bucket or a child's toy against the dust.

## 3 — Receiving Aid
*Message: "Your donation reaches real hands. It works."*

- **FOCUS** on the receiving face, not the giver, at the moment the parcel arrives.
- **CLOSE IN** on hands: bread passing from one hand to another, flour poured into a bowl, water filling a jug.
- **SHOOT** a child walking away from the distribution, clutching food to their chest, maybe with a small smile.
- **FRAME** a family opening an aid parcel together inside their tent.
- **CAPTURE** the community kitchen: steam, big pots, a ladle filling a child's bowl. Low angle, bowl in the foreground.
- **SHOW** relief: a mother's exhale, a child's first bite, eyes closed while drinking clean water.
- **AVOID** branded logos of *other* organisations dominating the frame (they can confuse donors), and avoid chaotic crowding.

---

## Before you send

- [ ] Vertical 9:16, at least 1080×1920
- [ ] No text, stamps, filters or watermarks on the image
- [ ] Eyes sharp
- [ ] Face is in the safe centre (not in the top 14% or the bottom third)
- [ ] Nothing graphic in frame (guide 04)
- [ ] Consent obtained, with name, age and a one-line story noted
- [ ] 3–5 variations per scene
